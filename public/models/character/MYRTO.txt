DESIGN
A fictional adult Athenian civilian, broadly inspired by Classical-period dress.
Myrto is a working character name, not an identified historical person. The goal
is a calm, approachable player-character silhouette rather than a goddess,
warrior, or generic white-marble statue. No weapon, laurel crown, fantasy armour,
or modern high heels. The face, hair, palette and modelling are original.

She wears an ochre, shoulder-pinned peplos with a visible upper overfold, simple
waist tie, long pleated skirt, sea-green mantle and low leather sandals. The
hem is raised slightly above the ankles for a readable walk and easier game
integration. The mantle and skinning are deliberately simplified for real-time
use. This is a historically informed interpretation, NOT a scholarly costume
reconstruction or a simulated garment made from a sewing pattern.

REFERENCE BASIS (consulted 29 October 2026)
1. Metropolitan Museum of Art, Department of Greek and Roman Art,
   “Ancient Greek Dress” (2003).
   https://www.metmuseum.org/essays/ancient-greek-dress
   Basis: rectangular garments, shoulder fastening, waist belting, overfold,
   colored cloth, diagonal mantle arrangements and sandals.
2. Harold Koda, Metropolitan Museum of Art,
   “The Chiton, Peplos, and Himation in Modern Dress” (2003).
   https://www.metmuseum.org/essays/the-chiton-peplos-and-himation-in-modern-dress
   Basis: structural distinction of the peplos, its overfold and shoulder pins;
   the himation as a rectangular mantle. Also cautions against treating the
   evidence for ancient dress as completely uniform or certain.
3. Dr Chrysanthi Tsouli, National Archaeological Museum, Athens,
   “A peplos for Athena …” (2017), sculpture inv. 1732, c. 440 BCE.
   https://www.namuseum.gr/en/monthly_artefact/a-peplos-for-athena/
   Basis: Classical-period peplos, fastening, belting and pleated drapery.
   The character is not a copy of the sculpture, and is not intended as Athena.

RIG / SCALE / AXES
• Units: metres. Approximately 1.76 m including gathered hair.
• Blender: Z-up, face points -Y. GLB: Y-up, face points +Z.
• Origin at ground centre. No root-motion translation is baked into the clips.
• 26-bone FK deform skeleton: root, pelvis, spine, chest, neck, head;
  clavicle/upper arm/forearm/hand on each side; thigh/shin/foot/toe on each side;
  four secondary skirt bones. Names use .L / .R conventions.
• Body and clothing have explicit skin weights. The full skirt is not bound
  independently to the two legs, so its hem can remain continuous.
• Idle: 3 seconds / 72-frame cycle at 24 fps.
• Walk: 1 second / 24-frame cycle at 24 fps.
• The duplicated closing key is present at frame 73 or 25 for seamless looping.
• In Blender, the two actions are saved with fake users and muted NLA tracks.
  Idle is active on opening. To preview Walk, select Myrto_Rig, choose Walk in
  the Action Editor and set the playback end to 24. Do not unmute both tracks.

EDITING / EXPORT
Source parts are deliberately separate and named. Change the 14 shared PBR
materials to recolor the asset. The included GLB combines these parts into one
skinned mesh with material primitives to reduce draw calls; this does not flatten
or remove the original editable parts in the .blend source.

To rebuild using the installed Blender version:
  blender -b -t 8 -P build_character.py
  blender -b -t 8 -P export_and_validate.py
The first script creates the source and still renders. The second optimises the
GLB, reimports it, validates skinning and looping, and renders the walk sequence.
Scripts write only beside themselves; keep a separate copy before experimenting.

REVIEW LIMITATIONS
• This is a reviewable prototype, not a final production character. Clothing
  folds, feet, hands and facial detail are intentionally stylised.
• No facial blendshapes, blinking, speech, fingers, IK control rig, collisions,
  LODs, retargeting profile, or cloth simulation. The hands move with hand bones.
• The simple FK walk is a small in-place movement, not foot-locked motion capture.
  Long garments and fast/extreme poses will need more weighting and pose testing.
• The source torso is a concealed support form, not a complete unclothed base mesh.
• Actual game-controller, gameplay-scale, performance, browser and lighting
  integration have not been tested. No existing game file was changed or deployed.
• All geometry and materials were generated for this request; no downloaded model,
  photographic texture, commercial asset or third-party character was reused.

CURRENT EXPORT BUDGET
The review GLB has one skinned mesh, 14 material primitives and 82,336 triangles
(about 3 MB). This prioritises review detail over a mobile-game triangle budget;
create a lower-detail variant after the visual direction and target are approved.
