Environment maps are loaded at runtime; the default points to the Poly Haven
`clear_noon_1k.exr` file to avoid bundling large binaries. To override it with a
local capture, drop a half-float equirectangular EXR in this folder and update
`hdrPath` in `src/core/Application.js`.

Convert a Radiance .hdr capture to the expected format with ImageMagick:

convert clear_midday.hdr -compress zip -define exr:pixelType=half clear_midday.exr

Ensure the output keeps a 2:1 aspect ratio for correct sky projection.
